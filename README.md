## 👋 About Me

I'm **Fábio França**, a Business Data Science student at FATEC Votorantim, Brazil, currently working as a Data Analytics Intern at **Frasle Mobility**.

My interests go beyond writing code. I enjoy understanding business problems, exploring data, developing analytical solutions and discovering how technology can improve decision-making.

I believe that good data analysis is not just about generating reports — it's about finding meaningful insights and turning them into real-world impact.

**Areas I'm particularly interested in:**

- 📊 Business Intelligence & Data Analytics
- 🤖 Machine Learning & Predictive Modeling
- 📈 Quantitative Finance & Financial Data
- ⚙️ Process Automation & Data Engineering
- 💡 Business Strategy & Data-Driven Decisions

## 💼 Professional Experience

**Data Analytics Intern — Frasle Mobility**

Working with data and analytical solutions in an industrial maintenance environment.

Some of the activities and solutions I've been involved in:
